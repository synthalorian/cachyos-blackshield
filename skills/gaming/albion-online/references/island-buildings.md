# Albion Island & Player-Building Management

Mechanics synth hits on his island (gathered Aug 2026 while removing an Expert Cook).

## Removing a building

- **Renovation → Demolish** removes the building and frees the plot. This is the only removal path for your own building.
- **"Give up"** (red button) only relinquishes ownership — the building stays standing. Not removal.
- **Demolish blockers** (button refuses / does nothing):
  1. Items inside the station — clear BOTH the crafting queue AND the output/inventory slots; unclaimed finished items block demolition.
  2. Active crafting jobs — cancel them first.
  3. Construction/upgrade in progress — let it finish or cancel it.
- **Lost on demolish:** everything in the Food Supply bar and any contents left inside. Pull valuables first; the food percentage is forfeited.

## Panel anatomy (for reading synth's screenshots)

- Three status bars up top: durability / food / (building-specific) — a near-empty pale bar is the food supply.
- "Downgrade" is often grayed out (only available within a window after upgrading).
- Owner vs Built-by can differ (synth's cook: Owner synthalorian, Built by DestinysShadow) — only Owner permissions matter for Demolish.
