# Sword Line + Bruiser Kit — Verified Game Data (ao-bin-dumps master, Aug 2026)

Item IDs, spell slot mappings, and current EN-US ability text for the sword line and the
standard melee-bruiser gear synth asked about. Re-pull from ao-bin-dumps after major patches.

## Sword weapon line (from T4_MAIN_SWORD craftingspelllist)

Core mechanic: **Heroic Charges** — each stack increases auto-attack speed (stacks to 3).
Generated by Heroic Strike (Q), Iron Will (W), and the Heroic Fighting passive. Consumed/
amplified by E abilities and Blade Cyclone.

### Q (slots="1")
- `HEROICSTRIKE2` = **Heroic Strike** — dash to target enemy, physical damage, +move speed,
  applies 1 Heroic Charge.
- `CLEAVE` = **Heroic Cleave** (unlock T2+)

### W (slots="2")
- `SWORD_SPIN` = **Blade Cyclone** — spin in target direction, hits all enemies passed through
  up to 3x; consumes 1 Heroic Charge for higher per-hit damage. 12s recast, 9m range dash.
- `INTERRUPT2` = **Interrupt** (T3+) — bash, interrupts casting; +auto-attack damage per Heroic Charge.
- `SPLITTINGSLASH` = **Splitting Slash** (T4+) — line skillshot, roots all enemies hit.
- `HAMSTRINGSWORD` = **Hamstring** — 2-part combo: (1) damage + slow, (2) Heroic Leap to a position with AoE damage.
- `PARRY` = **Parry Strike** — channeled damage immunity (can't be interrupted), reflects portion
  of incoming damage as magic, AoE swing at end.
- `DEFENSERUN` = **Iron Will** — +move speed, +defense, **purge immunity** while active, applies 1 Heroic Charge.

### E (slots="3") — per weapon
- Broadsword (`T4_MAIN_SWORD`): `MIGHTYBLOW` = **Mighty Blow** — leap to target, interrupts casting,
  physical damage scaling with number of Heroic Charges (0–3). Single-target execute.
- Dual Swords (`T4_2H_DUALSWORD`, removes MIGHTYBLOW): `SPINATTACK` = **Spinning Blades** — leap to
  position, **CC-immune until impact**, AoE damage on landing; hitting an enemy grants +auto-attack
  speed and +physical auto-attack damage.

### Passives
- `PASSIVE_BLEEDCHANCE` = **Deep Cuts** — every N auto-attacks, bleed DoT.
- `PASSIVE_REDUCE_DMG_SWORD` = **Weakening** (T2+)
- `PASSIVE_HEROICSTACK` = **Heroic Fighting** (T4+) — every N auto-attacks applies 1 Heroic Charge.
- `PASSIVE_ARMORCHANCE_SWORD` — every N auto-attacks, +damage resistances.

## Bruiser kit items (synth's broadsword build, verified)

- **Facebreaker** = `T4_OFF_SPIKEDSHIELD_MORGANA` — off-hand, NO active/passive spells; raw stats:
  +physical/magic spell damage bonus, +physical/magic attack damage bonus, +threat. Zero defense. Pure greed pick.
- **Demon Cape** = `T4_CAPEITEM_DEMON` → `PASSIVE_CAPE_DEMON` = **Opening to Hell** — on auto-attack,
  conjures a lava puddle around the target enemy dealing magic DoT (separate player/mob values).
- **Mercenary Hood** = `HEAD_LEATHER_SET1` — actives: `ENERGY_BARRIER` = Energizing Shield (shield +
  missing-energy restore), `HOWL` = Howl (cone: magic damage, slow, −damage dealt debuff, interrupts
  casting), `SELF_CLEANSE` = Cleanse (AoE circle cleanse of CC + debuffs on allies, usable while
  stunned/silenced, doesn't remove DoTs).
- **Hunter Jacket** = `ARMOR_LEATHER_SET2` — actives: `OUTOFCOMBATHEAL` = Mend Wounds (out-of-combat
  channel heal), `FLAMESHIELD` = Inferno Shield (+damage resistances, reflects portion as magic),
  `HASTE` = Haste (+auto-attack speed and +ALL damage). NOTE: Hunter Jacket's steroid is Haste —
  "Retaliate" is outdated/misremembered.
- **Soldier Boots** = `SHOES_PLATE_SET1` — includes `WANDERLUST` = Wanderlust (stacking move speed
  every pulse, holds at max stacks), also Dodge and Sprint HoT (`SPRINTHOT`) options.
- **Snapper Roast** = `T7_MEAL_ROAST_FISH` (Roasted Puremist Snapper) → `FOOD_FISH_BUFF_LIFESTEAL` —
  +max health; dealing damage steals a % of the health the enemy has LOST for a short window.
- **Poison Potion** = `T4_POTION_COOLDOWN` → `POTION_POISON` — throwable, true-damage DoT (separate
  player/mob values) and **reduces target's damage resistances** while poisoned.

## Build advice given (Aug 2026, holds while these spells do)

- Dual Swords group bruiser: Heroic Strike / Iron Will / Spinning Blades / Heroic Fighting;
  Soldier Helm (Block) or Merc Hood; Merc Jacket (Bloodlust); Soldier Boots (Wanderlust);
  Thetford cape for PvP, Demon for PvE; omelette (PvP) or stew (PvE); healing/resistance pots.
- Broadsword + Facebreaker = burst-assassin profile (delete one target); fix squish with Iron Will
  and Thetford cape swap for PvP zones. Snapper Roast lifesteal pairs with fast sword autos.
